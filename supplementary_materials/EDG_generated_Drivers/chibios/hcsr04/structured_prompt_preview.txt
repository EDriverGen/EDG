Connect the serial port. The external MCU or PC sends 0XA0. After measuring, the module returns three distance bytes: BYTE_H, BYTE_M, and BYTE_L.

Distance is calculated as follows (mm):

Distance=((BYTE_H<<16)+(BYTE_M<<8)+ BYTE_L)/1000

## Distance=T*C/2  (C is the speed of sound) [pages 4-5]

Speed of sound versus temperature: c=(331.45+0.61t/C)m*s-1 (330.45 at 0 C)

Speed of sound at 0 C: 330.45 M/S

Speed of sound at 20 C: 342.62 M/S

Speed of sound at 40 C: 354.85 M/S

The speed-of-sound variation from 0 C to 40 C is approximately 7%. Applications requiring precise distance values must account for temperature effects and apply temperature compensation.

## Overview: [pages 2-2]

The HC-SR04 (2020 version) is an open ultrasonic ranging module with the same dimensions as the earlier version and added UART and IIC functions. Its software and hardware are fully compatible with the earlier HC-SR04 by default; resistors can select UART or IIC mode. It features a 2 CM minimum blind zone, a typical maximum range of 4.5 M, and a 2.2 mA operating current. The upgraded RCWL-9206 demodulation chip and MCU with UART and IIC functions simplify peripheral circuitry, extend the supply range to 3-5.5 V, and provide more interfaces.

## Application notes: [pages 7-7]

1: Avoid connecting this module while powered. If a powered connection is necessary, connect Gnd first.

2: For irregular surfaces or distant objects, use repeated measurements for correction.

3: The interval between measurements must be at least 200 mS.

4: Installing the module inside a product enclosure may require adjustments to peripheral parameters;

contact the manufacturer to confirm the parameters before volume production.
